# Biology — Year 11 in 2027, HSC in 2028

## Full source

The complete NESA syllabus and syllabus support are saved as Word documents in this folder.

## Course notes

Use the 2025 Biology syllabus, including Year 11 and 12 content and course requirements. The first HSC for this syllabus is in 2028.
Year 11: cells as the basis of life, cells to systems, evolution and ecosystems. Year 12: heredity, diseases, biodiversity, biotechnology.
The 2017 syllabus document in this folder is an older version for reference; it does not apply to your cohort.
