# English Standard

## Full source

The complete NESA syllabus and syllabus support are saved as Word documents in this folder.

## Course notes

Year 11: Reading to write: Transition to English Standard; Contemporary possibilities; Close study of literature.
Year 12: Texts and human experiences; Language, identity and culture; Close study of literature; The craft of writing.
The 2024 syllabus starts with Year 11 in 2026, with the first HSC in 2027. Your texts depend on your school and the HSC prescriptions.
