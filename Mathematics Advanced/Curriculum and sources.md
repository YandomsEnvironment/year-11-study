## Course notes

Year 11: functions and graph transformations; trigonometry; exponential and logarithmic functions; introduction to differentiation; probability and data.
Year 12: further graph transformations and modelling; sequences and series; differential and integral calculus and applications; random variables; financial mathematics.
The 2024 syllabus starts with Year 11 in 2026; first HSC in 2027.
