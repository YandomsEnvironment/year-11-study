# Mathematics Extension 1

## Full source

The complete NESA syllabus and syllabus support are saved as Word documents in this folder.

## Course notes

Year 11: further functions; polynomials; further trigonometry; permutations and combinations; binomial theorem.
Year 12: mathematical induction; vectors; inverse trigonometric functions; further calculus and applications; binomial and sampling distributions.
The 2024 syllabus starts with Year 11 in 2026; first HSC in 2027. This course is studied alongside Mathematics Advanced.
