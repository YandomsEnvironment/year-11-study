# Physics — Year 11 in 2027, HSC in 2028

## Full source

The complete NESA syllabus and syllabus support are saved as Word documents in this folder.

## Course notes

Use the 2025 Physics syllabus, including Year 11 and 12 content and course requirements. The first HSC for this syllabus is in 2028.
Year 11: fundamentals of mechanics, waves, electricity and magnetism. Year 12: advanced mechanics, electromagnetism, nature of light, matter, energy and the cosmos.
The 2017 syllabus document in this folder is an older version for reference; it does not apply to your cohort.
